How to report a mistake

  1. Every ayah is labelled with its surah and ayah number, for example 2:27.
  2. Press the Report button next to the ayah. Your email app opens with the ayah already filled in.
  3. Describe what is wrong and what it should be. A photo of the printed mushaf helps.

The "Page" markers show where each page of the 604-page Madani Mushaf begins, to help compare with a printed copy.
